# KYC procedure: United Kingdom

1. Search the company register by name. Confirm the match using the registered office address and, where the prospect declared it, the company number.
2. Retrieve the company profile: name, number, status, date of creation, registered office, SIC codes.
3. Retrieve officers. Directors are officers with the role `director`; secretaries are not directors.
4. Retrieve persons with significant control (PSC). Ownership is reported in bands (25 to 50%, 50 to 75%, 75 to 100%). For corporate PSCs, look up the corporate entity by its registration number and continue up the chain.
5. Status must be `active`. `dissolved` or `liquidation` means the company is not active.
6. Search English-language news for the company name and its trading name.
7. Use the most recent annual report (turnover, profit for the year, going concern statement).
