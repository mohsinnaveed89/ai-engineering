# Hawksmoor Beacon Foods Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 03553268

## Strategic report

Hawksmoor Beacon Foods operates in manufacture of food products. Sales fell 1% to £32.9 million (2024: £33.3 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Cyber security is a growing risk. The company completed an external penetration test during the year.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | £32,949 | £33,299 |
| Profit/(loss) for the financial year | £3,634 | £3,280 |
| Total assets | £40,361 | £37,394 |
| Average number of employees | 289 | 269 |

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 5% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.
