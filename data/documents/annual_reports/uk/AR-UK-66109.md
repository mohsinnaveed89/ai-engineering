# Yarrow Gaming Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 06161365

## Strategic report

Yarrow Gaming operates in gambling and betting. Revenue rose 12% to £26.6 million (2024: £23.6 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £26,575 | £23,643 |
| Profit/(loss) for the financial year | £943 | £783 |
| Total assets | £32,844 | £33,341 |
| Average number of employees | 192 | 191 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 10% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.
