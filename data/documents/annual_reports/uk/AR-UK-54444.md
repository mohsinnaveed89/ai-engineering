# Harrowgate Lumen Engineering Limited
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Harrowgate Lumen Engineering operates in engineering activities. Revenue rose 1% to £1.3 million (2024: £1.3 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The principal risks facing the company are competition, customer concentration and the availability of skilled staff.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £1,284,000 | £1,269,000 |
| Profit/(loss) for the financial year | £83,000 | £105,000 |
| Total assets | £1,147,000 | £1,184,000 |
| Average number of employees | 11 | 10 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 12% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.
