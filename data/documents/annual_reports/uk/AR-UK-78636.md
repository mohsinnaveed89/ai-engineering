# Davenport Energy Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Davenport Energy Ltd operates in production of electricity. Turnover rose 5% to £83.7 million (2024: £80.0 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Turnover | £83,710 | £79,970 |
| Net profit/(loss) | £2,403 | £1,707 |
| Total assets | £114,985 | £118,816 |
| Average monthly headcount | 908 | 915 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 15% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Trading in the first months of the new year has been in line with the board's expectations.

## Independent auditor's report

We have audited the financial statements of Davenport Energy Ltd for the year ended 31 December 2025. In our opinion the financial statements give a true and fair view of the state of the company's affairs as at 31 December 2025 and of its profit for the year then ended.
