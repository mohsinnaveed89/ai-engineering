# Fenwick Meridian Foods PLC
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 06612969

## Strategic report

Fenwick Meridian Foods PLC operates in manufacture of food products. Sales rose 2% to £5.7 million (2024: £5.6 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The principal risks facing the company are competition, customer concentration and the availability of skilled staff.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | £5,701 | £5,610 |
| Net profit/(loss) | £746 | £652 |
| Total assets | £7,971 | £8,095 |
| Average monthly headcount | 43 | 37 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 8% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Trading in the first months of the new year has been in line with the board's expectations.
