# Glenrothes Energy Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 03133420

## Strategic report

Glenrothes Energy operates in production of electricity. Revenue fell 4% to £189.5 million (2024: £197.7 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Rising energy and input costs remain the most significant risk; the company has fixed prices for part of next year's supply.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £189,499,000 | £197,662,000 |
| Profit/(loss) after tax | £7,188,000 | £6,693,000 |
| Total assets | £154,865,000 | £152,755,000 |
| Average monthly headcount | 1410 | 1488 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 8% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.
