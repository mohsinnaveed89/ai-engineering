# Yarrow Digital Assets Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Yarrow Digital Assets Ltd operates in digital asset trading. Revenue fell 11% to £12.7 million (2024: £14.3 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £12,679,000 | £14,323,000 |
| Profit/(loss) after tax | £405,000 | £344,000 |
| Total assets | £9,212,000 | £9,724,000 |
| Average number of employees | 133 | 131 |

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 12% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.

## Independent auditor's report

We have audited the financial statements of Yarrow Digital Assets Ltd for the year ended 31 December 2025. In our opinion the financial statements give a true and fair view of the state of the company's affairs as at 31 December 2025 and of its profit for the year then ended.
