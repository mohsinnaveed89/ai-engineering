# Ashford Harbour Software PLC
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 13371114

## Strategic report

Ashford Harbour Software operates in software development. Revenue for the year was £2.6 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 7% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook
