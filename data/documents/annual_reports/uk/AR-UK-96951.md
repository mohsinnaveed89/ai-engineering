# Hawksmoor Crest Holdings Limited
## Group Strategic Report and Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 07622914

## Strategic report

Hawksmoor Crest Holdings is the holding company of a group operating in media production. Revenue for the year was £5.5 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

The Group's principal trading subsidiary, Fenwick Media Ltd, contributed revenue of £4.4 million. The figures below are for the Group as a whole.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Rising energy and input costs remain the most significant risk; the company has fixed prices for part of next year's supply.

### Environment and people

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Trading in the first months of the new year has been in line with the board's expectations.
