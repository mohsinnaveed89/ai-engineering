# Elmstead Software Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Elmstead Software operates in software development. Sales rose 10% to £30.4 million (2024: £27.6 million). The company made a loss for the year, reflecting lower volumes and higher input costs.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Cyber security is a growing risk. The company completed an external penetration test during the year.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | £30,366 | £27,577 |
| Profit/(loss) after tax | (£1,150) | £1,230 |
| Total assets | £34,292 | £34,780 |
| Average number of employees | 198 | 204 |

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 14% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Next year will focus on margin recovery and tighter working-capital management.
