# Greystone Anchor Holdings Limited
## Group Strategic Report and Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: SC101580

## Strategic report

Greystone Anchor Holdings is the holding company of a group operating in engineering activities. Sales for the year was £1.6 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

The Group's principal trading subsidiary, Harrowgate Lumen Engineering Limited, contributed revenue of £1.3 million. The figures below are for the Group as a whole.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 3% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Next year will focus on margin recovery and tighter working-capital management.
