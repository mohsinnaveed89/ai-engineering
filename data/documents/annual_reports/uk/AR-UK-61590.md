# Kingsmere Anchor Holdings Limited
## Group Strategic Report and Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Kingsmere Anchor Holdings is the holding company of a group operating in software development. Turnover for the year was £2.0 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

The Group's principal trading subsidiary, Redcliffe Meridian Software Limited, contributed revenue of £1.5 million. The figures below are for the Group as a whole.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Cyber security is a growing risk. The company completed an external penetration test during the year.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 17% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.
