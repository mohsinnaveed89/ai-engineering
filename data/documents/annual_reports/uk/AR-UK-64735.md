# Whitlock Software PLC
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: SC860077

## Strategic report

Whitlock Software operates in software development. Sales fell 12% to £862k (2024: £984k). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | £862 | £984 |
| Net profit/(loss) | £71 | £93 |
| Total assets | £1,441 | £1,554 |
| Average number of employees | 9 | 9 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 4% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

The board expects modest growth next year, supported by new contracts signed after the year end.

## Independent auditor's report

We have audited the financial statements of Whitlock Software PLC for the year ended 31 December 2025. In our opinion the financial statements give a true and fair view of the state of the company's affairs as at 31 December 2025 and of its profit for the year then ended.
