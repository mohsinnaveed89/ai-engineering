# Netherby Digital Assets Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 12001837

## Strategic report

Netherby Digital Assets operates in digital asset trading. Turnover for the year was £2.0 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Cyber security is a growing risk. The company completed an external penetration test during the year.
