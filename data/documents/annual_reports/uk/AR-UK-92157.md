# Greystone Vector Holdings Limited
## Group Strategic Report and Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 14980040

## Strategic report

Greystone Vector Holdings is the holding company of a group operating in manufacture of textiles. Revenue for the year was £99.1 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

The Group's principal trading subsidiary, Northcote Crest Textiles PLC, contributed revenue of £70.9 million. The figures below are for the Group as a whole.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Rising energy and input costs remain the most significant risk; the company has fixed prices for part of next year's supply.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 14% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Next year will focus on margin recovery and tighter working-capital management.
