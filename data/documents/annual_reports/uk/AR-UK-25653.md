# Marlow Harbour Pharma Limited
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025

Company registration number: 11706862

## Strategic report

Marlow Harbour Pharma operates in manufacture of pharmaceutical preparations. Revenue for the year was £7.6 million. The company made a loss for the year, reflecting lower volumes and higher input costs.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

The directors consider the main risks to be credit risk on trade receivables and the renewal of key contracts.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 3% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have identified a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt on the company's ability to continue as a going concern, related to the renewal of its banking facilities.

## Outlook

Next year will focus on margin recovery and tighter working-capital management.
