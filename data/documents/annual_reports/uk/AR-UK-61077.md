# Calder Summit Energy PLC
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025


## Strategic report

Calder Summit Energy operates in production of electricity. Revenue rose 11% to £89.3 million (2024: £80.7 million). Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Rising energy and input costs remain the most significant risk; the company has fixed prices for part of next year's supply.

### Key performance indicators

All figures in £'000.

| | 2025 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £89,331 | £80,739 |
| Profit/(loss) after tax | £8,685 | £8,773 |
| Total assets | £123,600 | £135,080 |
| Average monthly headcount | 1123 | 1114 |

### Environment and people

Staff turnover fell to 6% and the company introduced a new wellbeing programme.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Trading in the first months of the new year has been in line with the board's expectations.
