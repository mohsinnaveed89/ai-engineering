# Elmstead Software Ltd
## Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2024

Company registration number: SC893170

## Strategic report

Elmstead Software operates in software development. Sales for the year was £27.6 million. Profit for the year reflected tight cost control.

### Principal risks and uncertainties

Cyber security is a growing risk. The company completed an external penetration test during the year.

### Environment and people

The company reduced its Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 14% during the year and installed solar panels at its main site.

## Going concern

The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The financial statements are therefore prepared on the going concern basis.

## Outlook

Trading in the first months of the new year has been in line with the board's expectations.
